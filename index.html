How scoring works

  • 0 - Not implemented: we do not do this yet.
  • 1 - Partially implemented: we do this sometimes, or only in some places.
  • 2 - Largely implemented: we do this most of the time, with some gaps.
  • 3 - Fully implemented: we do this consistently.
  • 4 - Fully implemented and reviewed: we do this consistently and we review and improve it regularly.
  • N/A: this does not apply to us. N/A answers are left out of the score.

Each function score is the average of its answered questions, shown as a percentage out of 4. The overall score is the average of the six function scores.
